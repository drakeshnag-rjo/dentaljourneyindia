### DentalJourneyIndia — Partnership Overview

**Who we are.**
DentalJourneyIndia (dentaljourneyindia.org) is a patient-referral and concierge service for international dental patients. We market to patients in the US, UK, Canada, and Australia — where a single implant costs $3,000–$6,000 and full mouth rehabilitation runs $25,000–$50,000 — and connect them with verified clinics in Hyderabad, Vijayawada, and Guntur. Patients reach us through our website and our AI concierge on Telegram; we handle everything up to the chair.

**We are new, and we're saying so plainly.**
We are currently assembling our founding partner group — 2 to 3 clinics per city, no more. Founding partners get our full referral flow in their city, direct access to the founder, and input into how the service works. We would rather start honestly with a few good clinics than promise volume we haven't proven yet.

**What your clinic gets:**
- Qualified international patients at **zero acquisition cost** — no ad spend, no listing fees, no retainer.
- We handle the entire top of the funnel: initial inquiry, treatment interest and budget qualification, scheduling coordination, and travel/visa questions. Your team receives a patient who knows what they want, what it costs, and when they're arriving.
- Accurate, professional presentation of your clinic to a Western audience on our site and in patient communications.

**What we ask:**
- A **10–15% referral fee on completed treatment only.** No patient, no fee. No-show or cancellation: nothing owed.
- A current price list for international patients (we publish transparent ranges; itemized quotes stay between you and the patient).
- One English-speaking coordinator we can reach on WhatsApp or email.
- Permission to feature your clinic accurately on our website — real accreditations, real services, nothing embellished.

**What we do NOT ask:**
- No exclusivity — keep every other channel you have.
- No upfront or recurring fees of any kind.
- No long contract — a simple agreement either side can exit with notice.

**Next step.** A 20-minute call or a visit to your clinic — we visit every clinic before featuring it. Reply to this email or WhatsApp us anytime.

hello@dentaljourneyindia.org · https://dentaljourneyindia.org

## B) 60-Second Phone Script (First Call)

**Goal of this call:** get the WhatsApp number or email of the owner or clinic manager. Not a deal, not a meeting — just the right person's contact. Everything else is a bonus.

**1. Reception (get to the decision-maker):**
> "Hi, this is [name] calling from DentalJourneyIndia — we refer international dental patients from the US and UK to clinics in [city]. Could I speak with the owner or clinic manager for two minutes? ... If they're with a patient, no problem — what's the best time to call back, or could I get their WhatsApp so I can send a short overview?"

**2. Decision-maker (one-sentence pitch):**
> "Thanks for taking this, Doctor. In one sentence: we bring you qualified international patients — inquiry, qualification, and scheduling all handled — at zero cost to you unless treatment is actually completed, in which case we take a small referral fee."

**3. Preempt the obvious question:**
> "I'll be upfront — we're a new service, and we're selecting just two or three founding clinics in [city], which is exactly why I'm calling clinics with your reputation first."

**4. The ask (contact, not commitment):**
> "I don't need a decision on the phone. Can I send you a one-page overview on WhatsApp or email so you can look when it's convenient? What's the best number or address?"

**5. Close:**
> "Perfect — I'll send it today. I'm [name], DentalJourneyIndia. Thank you, Doctor."

**If they push back ("we already get foreign patients" / "what's the catch"):**
> "That's great — this adds a channel, costs nothing, and asks no exclusivity. The only money that ever moves is a referral fee after a patient completes treatment. If it never works, you've lost nothing. May I send the one-pager?"
