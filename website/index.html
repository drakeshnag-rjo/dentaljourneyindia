How We Verify Clinics

Accreditation Checked

We look for NABH or JCI accreditation — audits covering sterilization protocols, infection control, equipment maintenance, and patient safety procedures.

Credentials Verified

Dentists must be registered with the Dental Council of India. We check qualifications — a 5-year BDS degree, with specialists holding an additional 3-year MDS.

Good to Know

Common Questions

How much can I save on dental treatment in India?

Dental treatment in India typically costs 70–90% less than in the US, UK, or Australia. For example, a single dental implant costs $300 - $800 in India versus $3,000 - $6,000 in the US. Even after adding flights and accommodation, most patients save thousands of dollars.

Is dental tourism in India safe?

Yes — at the right clinic. Indian dentists complete a 5-year BDS degree (specialists add a 3-year MDS) and must be registered with the Dental Council of India. Look for NABH- or JCI-accredited clinics with modern sterilization standards. Our safety guide has a full 10-point checklist.

How long do I need to stay in India?

Most treatment plans fit a 7-day trip: examination and digital imaging in the first days, treatment appointments interspersed with rest days, and final fittings and a review before you fly home. Your exact schedule depends on your treatment plan, so build in 1–2 buffer days. See our complete tourism guide for a day-by-day breakdown.

How does the process work?

Tell us your dental needs and share any existing X-rays or dental records. You'll receive a preliminary treatment plan with pricing within 24 hours, then compare quotes from 2–3 verified clinics before confirming your plan and booking travel.

What does a quote or consultation cost?

Nothing — the quote is completely free and comes with no obligation. Tell us what you need and we'll send a personalized treatment plan with pricing.

Do I need a special visa for dental treatment in India?

India offers an e-Medical Visa for patients, and an e-Tourist Visa also works for dental trips. Both are applied for online and typically processed in 2–3 business days. The e-Medical Visa lets you bring one companion.
